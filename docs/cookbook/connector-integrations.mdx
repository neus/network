Give your assistant access to your accounts. Connect once, choose what it can do, and your assistant uses the right app when you ask.

## How it works

```
You pick an app → choose what it can do → sign in once → your assistant uses only what you allowed
```

You keep your sign-in. NEUS adds the authority check before any app runs.

## Connect an account

1. Open your **Profile** in NEUS.
2. Go to **Trust graph → Connections**.
3. Pick an app from the catalog.
4. Choose the capabilities you want to allow — for example, **Read email** and **Send email**, but not **Delete email**.
5. Click **Connect** and authorize in the provider's sign-in window.
6. Done. Your assistant can now use only the capabilities you selected.

You can connect more than one account per app (for example, two Gmail accounts). Each connection is separate.

## Choose capabilities

When you connect an app, you pick exactly what your assistant can do. For Gmail:

- **Read email** — fetch and search messages
- **Send email** — send and reply
- **Drafts** — create and manage drafts
- **Labels** — manage labels and filters
- **Delete** — move to trash (off by default)
- **Settings** — change account settings (off by default)

Only the capabilities you enable are available to your assistant. If you turn off **Send email**, your assistant cannot send email through that connection.

You can change capabilities any time. Removing a capability takes effect immediately. Adding a capability that needs broader provider permissions may require reconnecting.

## What you can connect

The catalog is live. Common apps include Gmail, Google Drive, Slack, GitHub, Notion, Linear, and Salesforce. Search the catalog for the app you need. New apps appear automatically.

## Ask your assistant

Once connected, just ask:

- "Read my latest emails"
- "Find the Q3 roadmap in my Drive"
- "Send a Slack message to the engineering channel"
- "Create a GitHub issue for this bug"

Your assistant finds the right app, checks your selected capabilities, and runs. If you haven't connected the app yet, it tells you to connect first.

## Permissions and control

Your assistant only does what you allow:

1. **Your selected capabilities** — the allow list you chose at connect time.
2. **Your permission settings** — sends, creates, and deletes go through your agent permissions. You can require approval for high-impact actions.

Every action is recorded as a saved result you can review.

You can disconnect any account at any time from **Profile → Trust graph → Connections**.

## Add a custom server

For apps that expose their own server URL, connect it directly:

1. Go to **Profile → Trust graph → Connections**.
2. Click **Add connection**.
3. Paste the server URL and API key if the server requires one.
4. Your assistant discovers the server's tools at runtime.

## Security

- NEUS never stores your provider sign-in tokens.
- The capabilities you select are enforced at execution time — your assistant cannot use a capability you did not enable.
- Every call is checked against your permission settings before it runs.
- Every action is saved as a verifiable result.
