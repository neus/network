## How it works

Verify once. Carry the proof. Enforce before action.

Frameworks run tools. Models think. NEUS checks identity, provenance, and authority before sensitive actions.

1. **Verify once.** Run a check through the hosted screen, MCP, or SDK.
2. **Carry the proof.** NEUS saves the result with its status, allowed use, and expiration.
3. **Enforce before action.** Connected apps require that proof before access, payment, or execution.

See the full flow: [How verification works](./verification/how-it-works) · [Proof lifecycle](./verification/lifecycle) · [Proof links and versioning](./learn/receipts-as-knowledge).

## Where NEUS sits

Login gets people in. Authority decides what they can do next. NEUS records that authority as a portable proof any connected app can check.

- Keep your sign-in, identity provider, payment, and agent framework.
- Add NEUS via SDK, API, or MCP. No replacement required.
- Wallet checks are optional. Work accounts, domains, social accounts, and passkeys all work.
