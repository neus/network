## Connect NEUS

Add the hosted MCP endpoint, click **Connect**, and reuse your profile, proofs, permissions, and private context in that client.

`https://mcp.neus.network/mcp`

Then ask:

> Reuse what I already have. Before a sensitive action, check my current proofs.

NEUS answers **Passed**, **Action needed**, or **Blocked**.

Continue with [MCP setup](./mcp/setup) and [MCP overview](./mcp/overview).

## Create, carry, and enforce a proof

1. **Verify once.** Run a check through the hosted screen, MCP, or SDK.
2. **Carry the proof.** NEUS saves the result with its current status and expiration when one applies.
3. **Enforce before action.** Connected apps require that proof before access, payment, or execution.

Read the full flow in [How verification works](./verification/how-it-works) and [Standards & interoperability](./learn/standards).

## Where NEUS sits

Sign-in gets the user in. Authority decides what they can do next. NEUS records the completed check as a portable proof another connected app can reuse.

- Keep your sign-in, identity provider, payment, and agent framework.
- Add NEUS via SDK, API, or MCP.
- Work accounts, domains, social accounts, and passkeys all work. Wallet checks are optional.

Browse [Solutions](https://neus.network/solutions).
