## Connect an AI assistant

**The portable trust harness for AI.** Add the hosted MCP endpoint, click **Connect**, and the same profile, checks, Portable Proofs, permissions, and private context are available in that client.

`https://mcp.neus.network/mcp`

Then ask:

> Before you take a sensitive action, use NEUS. Reuse what I already have.

NEUS answers **Passed**, **Action needed**, or **Blocked**. You now have a clear decision before the action runs.

Continue with [MCP setup](./mcp/setup) and [the NEUS trust stack for MCP](./mcp/overview).

## Create, carry, and enforce a proof

1. **Verify once.** Run a check through the hosted screen, MCP, or SDK.
2. **Carry the proof.** NEUS saves the result with its current status and expiration when one applies.
3. **Enforce before action.** Connected apps require that proof before access, payment, or execution.

Read the full flow in [How verification works](./verification/how-it-works) and [Standards & interoperability](./learn/standards).

## Where NEUS sits

Login gets them in. Authority decides what they can do next. NEUS records the completed check as a portable proof another connected app can reuse.

- Keep your sign-in, identity provider, payment, and agent framework.
- Add NEUS via SDK, API, or MCP. No replacement required.
- No wallet required. Work accounts, domains, social accounts, and passkeys all work. Wallet checks are optional.

See how teams put this to work in [Solutions](https://neus.network/solutions).

## Release status

NEUS is under active development. Published APIs and verifier schemas may change between minor versions before the long-term stability freeze. We version everything we ship and document every breaking change in the [changelog](https://github.com/neus/network/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).
