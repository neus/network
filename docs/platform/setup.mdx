A **listing** is how you sell access from your NEUS profile: choose which checks visitors must pass, set the price, publish once, and embed with one public `gateId`. Sign-in and checks run on NEUS. Your app never handles wallets or keys.

## Steps

1. Sign in at [neus.network](https://neus.network).
2. Open your profile → **Listings** ([`?tab=portals`](https://neus.network/profile?tab=portals)) and create a listing.
3. Choose the checks visitors must pass and set pricing. You pay for checks by default, or charge visitors.
4. **Publish** and copy your **checkout link** or **embed snippet**.

Checks bill your NEUS account automatically because you own the gate. Visitors only need your `gateId`.

```jsx
import { VerifyGate } from '@neus/sdk/widgets';

<VerifyGate gateId="gate_your-app-name">
  <ProtectedContent />
</VerifyGate>
```

Full walkthrough: [Quickstart](../quickstart).

**Optional:** [Domain verification](../cookbook/domain-verification) confirms DNS control for profile trust and team workspace. It is separate from gates and not required to ship VerifyGate.
